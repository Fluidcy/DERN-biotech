About Dern Biotech

Technology designed around people.

We believe innovation should do more than push boundaries — it should help people live with greater confidence, comfort, and choice.

Dern Biotech is developing a vision for human-support technology that brings together protective concepts and mobility assistance. Our focus is on people whose movement may be limited by age, injury, or physical conditions, while recognising that every person's needs and goals are different.

Our technologies and descriptions on this website are conceptual until verified otherwise. Any implantable armour or powered mobility device would require rigorous engineering, safety testing, and relevant regulatory approval before use.
Our technology vision

Two ideas. One human purpose.

Our proposed technology areas aim to address different challenges: protection and physical mobility.

01 / PROTECTIVE TECHNOLOGY

Dern Armour

A long-term research concept exploring advanced protective materials and body-integrated protection.

  • Research into lightweight protective materials
  • Comfort, compatibility, and long-term safety as design priorities
  • Exploration of future body-integrated protection concepts
Subdermal implants are a highly complex medical concept and are not presented here as an available or proven product.
02 / MOBILITY TECHNOLOGY

Dern Exo

A mobility-support concept exploring wearable exoskeletons that may assist people with movement in everyday environments.

  • Support for standing, walking, or selected movements
  • Adjustable, user-centred fit and comfort
  • Designed around dignity, accessibility, and independence
Capabilities depend on the design and would need to be validated through appropriate testing and professional assessment.
